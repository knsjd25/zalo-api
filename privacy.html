Fruit Match

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Fruit Match (“we”, “our”, or “the Game”) handles information when you use our browser-based H5 match-3 puzzle game.

Fruit Match is a casual single-player puzzle game designed to be played directly in a web browser, mobile browser, or in-app browser.

2. Information We Collect

Fruit Match is designed as a lightweight game. In the basic version, we do not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information.

We may collect limited technical or gameplay-related information only when necessary to operate and improve the Game, such as:

  • Browser type and device type
  • Approximate access time
  • Basic gameplay information, such as score, moves, or game session status
  • Error logs or performance information
  • Referral source, such as whether the Game was opened from a shared link or social platform

3. Information We Do Not Collect

In the basic single-player version, we do not intentionally collect:

  • Real name
  • Phone number
  • Government ID
  • Precise location
  • Payment information
  • Contact list
  • Private messages
  • Sensitive personal information
The basic version of Fruit Match does not require Facebook Login or any social media account login.

4. How We Use Information

We may use limited technical or gameplay information to:

  • Provide and operate the Game
  • Improve gameplay experience and performance
  • Detect bugs, crashes, or technical issues
  • Understand general usage trends
  • Support sharing features
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the Game

We do not sell users’ personal information.

5. Local Storage

The Game may use browser local storage to save simple game status, such as:

  • Score
  • Remaining moves
  • Basic settings
  • Temporary gameplay progress

This information is stored on your device or browser. You can clear it by clearing your browser data or website storage.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The basic version of the Game does not require cookies for account login.

If analytics, advertising, or social sharing features are added in the future, cookies or similar technologies may be used to measure usage, improve performance, or support those third-party services.

7. Third-Party Services

The Game may be accessed through third-party platforms such as Facebook, social media apps, mobile browsers, or external websites. These platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If we use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, hosting, or sharing tools, those providers may process limited technical information under their own policies.

Possible third-party service categories may include:

  • Web hosting providers
  • Analytics tools
  • Advertising networks
  • Social sharing platforms
  • Error monitoring tools

8. Facebook or Meta Features

The basic version of Fruit Match does not require Facebook Login.

If we later add Facebook Login, Facebook sharing, or other Meta platform features, we may receive limited information that you choose to share through those features, such as public profile information or email address, depending on the permissions requested and approved.

We will only use such information to provide the related feature, such as login, sharing, or account identification.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Game
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the security, rights, or integrity of the Game
  • With your consent, such as when you choose to share content through a social platform

10. Data Retention

For the basic single-player version, most gameplay data is stored locally in your browser and remains there until you clear it.

If server-side data is created, such as a shared game link or gameplay record, we retain it only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the feature or maintain the service.

11. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of any data associated with your use of the Game.

To request data deletion, please contact us and include the following information if applicable:

  • Game Name: Fruit Match
  • Request Type: Data Deletion
  • Your related user ID, share ID, or email address if applicable

If you accessed the Game through Facebook or another platform, you may also remove the app or website connection from your platform account settings.

If the Game does not store any account data about you, we will inform you that there is no server-side personal data to delete.

12. Children’s Privacy

Fruit Match is a casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete such information.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the Game and any information processed through it.

However, no internet service or electronic storage method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. International Users

The Game may be accessed from different countries or regions. By using the Game, you understand that information may be processed in the location where our hosting or service providers operate.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Effective Date” at the top of this page. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
