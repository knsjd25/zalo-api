Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fruit Match handles information when you play the game through a mobile browser, Facebook in-app browser, or other WebView environment.

Information We Collect

The basic version of Fruit Match does not require account registration or login. We may process technical information that your browser normally sends when loading a web page, such as device type, browser type, approximate language settings, and page request information.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require your name, Facebook profile, contact list, payment information, or precise location to play the basic version of Fruit Match. We do not ask for Facebook Login, Graph API permissions, App Secret, or Facebook access tokens.

Local Storage

Fruit Match may use browser local storage to save simple game information such as score, progress, preferences, or recent play state. This information is stored on your device and can usually be cleared through your browser settings.

Cookies

The basic game does not require cookies for account login. Hosting providers, browsers, or third-party platforms may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Third-Party Services

You may access Fruit Match through third-party platforms, including Facebook or Meta products. Those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies. Please review the relevant platform privacy terms for details.

Facebook or Meta Features

Fruit Match is designed to be available in Facebook in-app browser and WebView contexts. The basic version does not integrate Facebook Login, Graph API, or Facebook permissions. If future features use platform identifiers, we will update this policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited technical information with hosting, security, analytics, or platform providers when needed to operate and protect the game.

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or your browser removes it. Server logs, if any, are retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, debugging, and service operation.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of server-side personal data by following the instructions on our Data Deletion Instructions page. If the basic version has no server-side account data related to you, we may explain that there is no server-side personal data to delete.

Children’s Privacy

Fruit Match is intended for general entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it when applicable.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the game and related systems. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we avoid collecting unnecessary personal information in the basic version.
